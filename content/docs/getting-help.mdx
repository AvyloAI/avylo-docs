# Getting Help & Support

Welcome to the **Avylo Private Alpha**. As an early-access builder, reviewer, or design partner, your direct feedback actively shapes the platform.

---

## Private Alpha Support Channels

### 1. Direct Founder & Alpha Support
If you have an assigned alpha contact or dedicated support channel, please reach out directly through your onboarding communications.

### 2. In-App Feedback
Inside the Avylo workspace, click the **Feedback** button in the top navigation to submit quick bug reports, usability notes, or feature requests directly to the engineering team.
