# Workspace Overview

The **Architecture Workspace** is the core operational cockpit in Avylo. It provides a persistent, real-time environment where you inspect your architecture model, collaborate with the AI Architect, and manage architectural evolution.

## Workspace Layout & Key Surfaces

The workspace is organized into four integrated surfaces:

### 1. The Interactive Architecture Canvas (Center)
The visual representation of your Canonical Architecture Model.
- **Node Graph**: Displays services, databases, queues, storage, and external providers with distinct visual cues.
- **Domain Boundaries**: Visually clusters components by business domain.
- **Connection Lines**: Represents network protocols, data flows, and dependencies.
- **Interactive Controls**: Pan, zoom, fit to screen, and filter views by domain or component type.

### 2. The AI Architect & Decisions Panel (Left / Drawer)
The reasoning interface where the AI Architect presents:
- **Active Recommendations**: Upcoming architecture decisions or optimizations.
- **Open Decisions**: Architectural forks awaiting your review.
- **Assumptions**: Pending AI inferences you can accept or customize.
- **Proposals & Change Sets**: Previews of proposed structural adjustments.

### 3. Properties & Traceability Inspector (Right)
When you select any component, decision, or capability:
- **Details**: Name, type, technology recommendation, and description.
- **Traceability Linkages**: Explicit connections showing which requirements and capabilities this component fulfills.
- **Related Decisions**: The exact Architecture Decisions that created or modified this component.
- **Health Indicators**: Specific health and confidence metrics for this node.

### 4. Activity Timeline (Bottom / Drawer)
A human-readable, chronological record of every architecture mutation:
- Decision approvals.
- Change Set applications.
- Project Knowledge updates.
- Milestone snapshots.

<Callout type="tip" title="Replacing Ephemeral Chat">
Unlike chatbot interfaces where crucial reasoning is lost in a long scrollable chat transcript, Avylo records all architectural history in the structured **Activity Timeline** attached directly to your project.
</Callout>
