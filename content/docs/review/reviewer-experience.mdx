## What Reviewers Can Do

### 1. Interactive Architecture Exploration
Reviewers can pan, zoom, inspect component topologies, and filter by domain without worrying about accidentally moving boxes or breaking connections.

### 2. Deep Decision Review
Reviewers can click on any component to read the exact **Architecture Decision** that created it:
- See the primary recommendation and evaluated alternatives.
- Inspect the trade-off matrix (complexity, cost, performance).
- Verify the grounded evidence citing business requirements.

### 3. Submitting Structured Feedback
Rather than sending vague messages over Slack or email, reviewers can attach contextual feedback directly to nodes or decisions:
- **Questions**: Request clarification on ambiguous constraints.
- **Risk Highlights**: Flag potential security, scaling, or operational pitfalls.
- **Alternative Proposals**: Suggest alternative technologies or architectural patterns for the creator's consideration.

---

## How Creators Process Reviewer Feedback

When a reviewer submits feedback:
1. The creator receives an alert in their **Activity Timeline**.
2. If the reviewer suggested an alternative pattern, the AI Architect analyzes the proposal's blast radius and presents a comparative **Change Set**.
3. The creator can either accept the proposal (which applies the change to the canonical model) or reply with clarifying context.
